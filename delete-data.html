Delete your BlockStruck data

BlockStruck: Block Puzzle Game, by Chimuanya Obi (BlockStruck), Nigeria

BlockStruck has no accounts. Your game data on our server is linked only to a random player ID made by the app. To have it deleted, send us that ID.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Open BlockStruck and tap Settings.
  2. Tap your player ID to copy it. If you made a backup, also note your restore code.
  3. Email blockstruck.game@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data and paste your player ID (and restore code, if you have one).
  4. We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

If you no longer have the app, email us anyway with your leaderboard name and roughly when you played, and we will do our best to find your data.

What is deleted

  • Your leaderboard entries (daily, weekly and all-time) and your display name.
  • Your progress backup and its restore code.
  • Your VS Friend race records (these are also deleted automatically after 48 hours).
  • Game statistics stored under your player ID.

What is kept

  • Daily totals that no longer contain your player ID (for example "1,000 games played today"), kept up to 400 days. They cannot identify you.
  • Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes it.
  • Advertising and app statistics data held by Google (AdMob and Firebase) is kept under Google's privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Full details: BlockStruck Privacy Policy.
